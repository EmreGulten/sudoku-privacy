How to play

Fill the grid so that every row, column and box contains each number only once. Choose a cell, then tap a number. Use Notes (pencil) to mark candidates, Undo to revert, Erase to clear, and Hint when you're stuck.

Frequently asked questions

  • Is my progress saved? Yes — your game auto-saves on your device after every move. Use "Continue" on the home screen to resume.
  • How does the Daily Challenge work? Every day a new puzzle is generated — the same one for everyone — and it refreshes the next day.
  • How do hints work? A free hint finds a cell with only one possible number and explains why. After your free hints run out, you can watch a short ad for more.
  • How do I unlock new themes? Go to the Theme Shop from the home screen. Locked themes (Neon, Wood) unlock by watching a short ad.
  • How do I reset my statistics? Statistics are stored only on your device. Reinstalling the app resets them.
  • Does it need internet? No. The game is fully offline. Only ads require a connection.

Troubleshooting

  • Ads not loading: Check your internet connection. On iOS, ads are limited by your tracking preference (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • App feels slow: Close other apps and restart Sudoku. Make sure iOS is up to date.
  • Lost my game: Progress is device-local and cannot be restored after uninstalling. We're working on optional cloud sync.

Compatibility

Requires iOS 15.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad.
